These require original photography — the company's own photos or licensed alternatives.

1. **Hamar woman at Dimeka market** (`omo-valley-hamar.webp`, 900×1200)
   - Shot: Environmental portrait, Hamar woman, ochre-butter braids, Key Afer or Dimeka Thursday market, bright fabrics, dry acacia in background. Late afternoon light preferred.
   - Do NOT substitute a generic stock image of African people.

2. **Hero background — Simien escarpment** (if company's own photo is higher quality)
   - Shot: Full-bleed landscape, golden hour, looking north toward Ras Dashen, escarpment edge visible, foreground grass, deep blue sky. 2400×1350 minimum.

3. **Lalibela interior — Beta Giyorgis or Bete Maryam**
   - Shot: Interior of a Lalibela church, priest or worshipper, candlelight, stone walls. Portrait orientation. For hero overlay slot.

4. **Coffee ceremony — hands pouring from jebena**
   - Shot: Close-up of hands pouring coffee from a clay jebena pot into small cups, charcoal embers, incense smoke. For Voices section and easter egg.

5. **Erta Ale lava lake at night**
   - Shot: Lava lake glow, rim in silhouette, stars visible. For Danakil scene hero. High ISO required.
